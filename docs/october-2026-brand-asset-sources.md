# October 2026 brand update

Added Hug Me First, Peachie Prep, and little eight; removed Eight Thousand Miles,
Beet World, ML Kids, Sawyer & Spade, and Yoga Baby from the showroom and featured
rotation. Yo Baby is a separate brand and remains listed.

All added photos are real brand photography saved locally as WebP at quality 92.
The existing image generation step creates responsive delivery sizes. No stock
or generated imagery is used. No wholesale ordering URLs were supplied for the
new brands, so their quick views use the existing Ordering Help contact action.
